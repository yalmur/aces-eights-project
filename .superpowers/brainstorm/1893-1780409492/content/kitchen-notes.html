How chips work

A

Toggle chips (recommended)

Each chip is independent. Click to select (oxblood fill), click again to deselect. Selected chips show as comma-separated text in the free-text field. Customer can still edit the text directly.

B

Chips just pre-fill text

Clicking a chip appends its label to the textarea. No selected state on the chip itself. Simple but chips don't deselect cleanly.
